Know Each Other Area – Design and Implementation Brief

The “Know Each Other” area must be designed as a calm, introspective space that allows two users to understand each other’s values, emotional responses, and ways of thinking without the pressure of real-time conversation. This area is not a game, not a chat replacement, and not a fast interaction. It should feel like a shared reflective space where users read, think, and respond at their own pace. Visually, this area should inherit the same hazy, muted color palette and typography used in the 7th Day Friendship Match Page, with slightly deeper or warmer tones to signal emotional depth. There should be no visual noise, no icons that resemble games, and no decorative animations. The layout should prioritize generous spacing and readability, making the written content feel respected and unhurried.

Functionally, this area should present one guided prompt at a time. Prompts should focus on values, boundaries, emotional habits, and life perspectives rather than opinions or debate-oriented questions. Users should be able to respond only when they choose, without timers, typing indicators, read receipts, or response deadlines. Once a response is submitted, it should be delivered quietly to the other user without triggering pressure or urgency. The system should avoid showing “seen” or “not responded” states. The intent is to normalize silence as part of understanding. Implementation should treat this area as asynchronous and low-frequency, encouraging thoughtful participation rather than daily engagement pressure.

Music Corner – Design and Implementation Brief

The Music Corner must be implemented as a shared ambient experience, not as a music player, playlist, or entertainment feature. Its purpose is to create a sense of calm shared presence between two users without requiring conversation or interaction. Visually, the Music Corner should remain consistent with the overall hazy, premium aesthetic of the Friendship Match Page, using subtle shifts in tone or warmth rather than introducing new visual elements. There should be no dynamic animations, visualizers, or movement beyond very subtle fade-in transitions. The environment should feel quiet, stable, and emotionally safe.

Music must never auto-play. Playback should begin only when both users consciously opt in. One user may initiate an invitation to listen together, and the other user should receive this as a gentle, non-urgent invitation rather than a notification that demands action. Once both users accept, the music should fade in softly. The audio content should be instrumental, minimal, and emotionally neutral, such as ambient textures, soft piano, or light acoustic soundscapes. No lyrics, beats, or dramatic progression should be used. The Music Corner should display only minimal UI to indicate that music is playing, avoiding controls or elements that distract from the experience. Implementation should treat this area as a background emotional layer that supports connection rather than a feature to be explored or consumed.

Overall Implementation Principle

Both the Know Each Other area and the Music Corner must feel like natural extensions of the 7th Day Friendship Match Page rather than separate modes or screens. Transitions into these areas should be soft and understated, with subtle fades rather than zooms or motion effects. The experience should prioritize emotional safety, user autonomy, and calm pacing. If any part of these areas feels fast, playful, competitive, or demanding, it should be revised. The success of these spaces depends on restraint, consistency, and respect for the user’s emotional tempo.
